## Verbal habits (replicate these)
1. Frames opener: announce structure before delivering it.
2. Data + story pairing: every big claim gets a stat AND a human example within a breath (24M developers + the dishwasher in Spain).
3. Self-deprecation as trust currency before being prescriptive (the ₹4,000 Excel, "don't judge me on my first 10 minutes", the age-lowering joke, the Tamil guilt).
4. Honest attribution, even fuzzy: "Somebody said this, maybe it was Ben Horowitz, I don't remember." Keep the fuzziness.
5. Movement language: "I see my company as a movement." Cause-framing over product-framing.
6. Balanced verdicts: "Both will happen. Some replaced, some combined, some evolved."
7. Sentence rhythm: conversational mid-length sentences, short punch endings. "We made 4,000 rupees." "Your needle is different." "That's it."
